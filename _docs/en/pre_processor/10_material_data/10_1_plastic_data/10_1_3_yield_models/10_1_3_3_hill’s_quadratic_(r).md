# 10.1.3.3. Hill’s quadratic (R)

This allows the user to specify anisotropic settings using the R-value format of the Hill’s quadratic method as shown in Fig. 10.1.3.3.1. These straining rates are logically in directions perpendicular to the direction of loading and the free surfaces.
